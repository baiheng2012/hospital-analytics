# Requirements and KPI Definitions

> Portfolio project on synthetic data (Synthea). The stakeholder scenario below is
> hypothetical and written to demonstrate requirements gathering; no real hospital was involved.

## 1. Business context (hypothetical)

A hospital senior leadership team wants a monthly operations view covering inpatient
length of stay, 30-day readmissions and emergency department (ED) activity, with
clear definitions so numbers can be reproduced and audited.

## 2. Scope

| In scope | Out of scope |
|---|---|
| Inpatient length of stay (LOS) | Departmental / service-line workload (source has no specialty data) |
| 30-day all-cause readmission | Planned vs unplanned readmission split |
| ED volume, ED LOS | Real triage / wait-to-physician times |
| Simulated time to physician initial assessment (PIA), clearly labelled | Costing, staffing |

Analysis period: **2017-2025 (complete years)**. 2026 is partial and excluded from KPIs.
Records before 2017 are sparse and excluded (about 58% of inpatient episodes fall before 2017).

## 3. KPI definitions

### 3.1 Inpatient LOS
| Item | Definition |
|---|---|
| Unit of analysis | Episode of care: inpatient encounters of the same patient are merged when the next admission starts within 24 hours of the latest discharge so far (handles overlaps and transfers such as ICU to ward) |
| Formula | (discharge datetime - admission datetime) in hours / 24 |
| Reported as | Median, mean, P90 (distribution is right-skewed; mean alone overstates a typical stay) |
| Period assignment | Discharge year |
| Denominator | 1,050 episodes (2017-2025) |
| Source objects | `dw.v_inpatient_episode` -> `dw.v_kpi_inpatient_los` |

Why merge: 2,942 raw inpatient encounters collapse to 2,761 episodes. Without merging,
admissions are overstated by about 7% and mean LOS is understated (4.79 vs 5.09 days on all years).

### 3.2 30-day all-cause readmission rate
| Item | Definition |
|---|---|
| Numerator | Index episodes followed by another inpatient episode of the same patient within 0-30 days of discharge |
| Denominator | Inpatient episodes (discharges), excluding patients who died before/at discharge |
| Attribution | The index (earlier) discharge |
| Reported as | Per year with numerator/denominator, plus pooled 2017-2025 rate (97 / 1,030 = 9.4%) |
| Source objects | `dw.v_readmission` -> `dw.v_kpi_readmission` |

Why the LOS and readmission denominators differ: 1,050 vs 1,030. The 20 episodes in
which the patient had died by discharge stay in LOS but cannot be readmitted.

### 3.3 ED metrics
| Metric | Definition | Status |
|---|---|---|
| ED visits | Encounters with class `emergency` | Real |
| ED LOS (minutes) | Stop minus start time; visits over 72 hours flagged `is_outlier` and excluded from LOS statistics | Real |
| PIA (minutes) | Time to physician initial assessment generated by a deterministic model (hour of day, weekday, season factors, hash-based noise) | **SIMULATED** |

Outliers: 112 ED visits overall exceed 72 hours; 27 fall in 2017-2025 (0.6% of visits). They are
flagged, not deleted, so row counts reconcile to the source.

## 4. Known data limitations

1. Synthea is synthetic and US-style; results do not describe any real hospital.
2. **Organization type is inferred from names** by keyword rules, and Synthea assigns encounters to
   organizations regardless of type (e.g. inpatient stays at outpatient clinics, ED visits at nursing
   homes). KPIs are therefore not sliced by organization.
3. All 1,024 providers have the specialty GENERAL PRACTICE, so department-level analysis is not possible.
4. ED LOS has little resolution: about 84% of ED visits last exactly 60 minutes (generator behaviour).
5. ED arrival times are close to uniform across hours; there is no real daytime peak.
6. PIA is simulated; its day/night and winter patterns are assumptions built into the model, not findings.
7. Readmission is all-cause; planned readmissions cannot be separated, so the rate may overstate
   unplanned readmission and should not be benchmarked directly against published indicators.
8. Annual readmission denominators are about 90-160, so single-year rates (5%-14%) are statistically noisy.
   Use the pooled rate as the baseline.

## 5. Data quality controls

- Staging layer keeps raw text; typing and cleaning happen in the warehouse layer.
- Post-load reconciliation checks (`sql/12_reconciliation.sql`) run at the end of every pipeline run:
  fact rows equal staging rows, no unknown dimension keys, inpatient episodes account for all raw
  inpatient records, and fact class keys match the staging class for every encounter.
- Checks were validated by deliberately corrupting one row and confirming the pipeline failed.
- Direct identifiers (SSN, driver's licence, passport, names, street address, coordinates) are not
  loaded into the warehouse layer (data minimisation).
